Finally, a woman! 🎉

For a sequel, it's good, but almost no sequel can meet the originality of the first part, and this book isn't an exception. I missed the frequent zooming in and out between the "big picture" and situations/dialogues that are explained in detail. The first part was full of that (I really liked that about the Three Body Problem, too). 3/5 stars.
