Quick, really nice read. I found the behavior of the main character (April) awfully annoying sometimes, which normally makes it hard for me to enjoy a book. I also would have loved to understand the Carls/the Dream more from an "expert"/hard sci-fi kind of view (what did scientists try to figure them out; what were their theories). But that writing is just super enjoyable – it's a pageturner. I started without any expectations and was pleasantly surprised.
