Today I restructured a LOT. After starting to read yesterday evening what I had written in the past months, it was necessary. Lots of things were mentioned early but should have been mentioned later, and the other way round. And I didn’t start the book with enough insights. 

But that’s all different now. I first tried finding a solution to the problems with post-its, but quickly figured out that this was fairly inefficient (or my handwriting was too bad). So I copied the long, long index I have in a new document and wrote down all the problems my old structure had – and then I just started moving things around and try to solve one problem after the other. And I’m happier now. Let’s see for how long.
