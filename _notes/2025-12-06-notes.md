My son has these great crayons. They make it super easy to draw big areas quickly…and make it impossible to draw precisely. Indeed, the pencil sharpener for these crayons doesn’t let you truly sharpen them. They’re like „Nope, there’s no way you’ll draw a precise line with these crayons. We won’t let you. The world is messy and best captured with imperfect strokes. Deal with it.“
