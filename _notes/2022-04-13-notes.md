QGIS, a geo software

More gradients today! I added two more sections to the “colors for gradients” chapters, and then freshed up the “classed vs unclassed” and “diverging vs sequential” debate. In total, I worked through **15 pages.**

![border](/pic/notes/rule6_copy_15.png)

Now I have a big-ish explanation on interpolation left. [I wrote one in 2014 on classed color scales](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://lisacharlottemuth.com/2014/12/14/Styling-Choropleth-Maps-2/&sa=D&source=docs&ust=1649867327335928&usg=AOvVaw3mIp1X0mTtP5uOypE-hH-v) and [one for Datawrapper (probably 2018?) on unclassed color scales](https://academy.datawrapper.de/article/117-color-palette-for-your-map) – and now I need to combine the two of them nicely, make the figures nicer, update the explanations – all of that. Which is exciting! But these figures are a bit more elaborate, and I can’t produce them with Adobe Illustrator or Datawrapper. I’ll need more elaborate tools like R and [QGIS](https://www.qgis.org/en/site/). 

So today I opened QGIS for the first time after...probably years? Definitely many months. That was nice. I like playing around with software.
