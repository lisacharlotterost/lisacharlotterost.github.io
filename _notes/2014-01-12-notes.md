**I love structure.** Structure and organisation makes me look back at the end of my days and lets me think: “Nice, today I did some productive stuff.” I recommend it. But what I found on the [Tumblr of “Fairy Tales for twenty-Somethings”](http://fairytalesfor20somethings.tumblr.com/page/2) was a organisation of daily life that blow me away. As a structure-junky I’m fascinated. But I’m not sure if I want to make plans what I could write on my two “Daily Schedule” pages. I think the right sense of setting borders for a day is important. One resolution for my master thesis writing time is to try different approaches in this direction.
