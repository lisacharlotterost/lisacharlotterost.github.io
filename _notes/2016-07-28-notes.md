**Portland, day 3! **Started again with Pre-Breakfast at “Simple. Local. Coffee.”; this time: Raspberry-Lime Scone. Delicious. Worked on a possible illustrative day-project idea. A few hours later all the fellows had a wonderful breakfast at Mother’s; and we drove to a waterfall where we hiked for ca. an hour before we got to the top of the mountain and therefore to a wonderful stream of wonderful fresh, clear water. I had lots of fun. 

Then: Driving back to Portland, late lunch at Whole Foods, writing in our fellow journals.
