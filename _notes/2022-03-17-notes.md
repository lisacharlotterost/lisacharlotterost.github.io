Today was a great day! I worked on all the small remaining stuff that needs to get done when you’re about to publish a massive blog post: Creating a feature image. Making sure the figures still make sense and adjusting where necessary. Resort thoughts and chapters and paragraphs into the most useful order. Creating good transitions between bigger parts. 

I also had a helpful interview with three(!) people (at the same time) (from one organization) in which I learned more about how they design for print. Printing colors is basically a dark art for me – I know very little about it, I should know more about it, and I will need to read more up on this.
