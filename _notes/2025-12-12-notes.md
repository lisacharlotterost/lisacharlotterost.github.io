**[Datawrapper has a new website!](https://www.datawrapper.de/)** Very, very exciting. It’s shiny and new and interactive; there are videos showing how Datawrapper works and toggles to click to change the appearance of charts. There are nice entryways to the [blog](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog), the [Datawrapper knowledge base](https://academy.datawrapper.de/), and much more. I especially like the **[overview of all the ways to learn Datawrapper and data visualization](https://www.datawrapper.de/learn)**.
