Today I did some research into how to create a color palette. Like, what should (or can) you do, exactly, when you sit down and try to come up with a few nice colors that you can use for e.g. a bar chart or line chart…and have no clue how to go about it? 

While doing that research, I stumbled upon a color palette generator I had no idea existed (but it’s such a good and obvious idea!): The [**Accessible color palette generator**](https://venngage.com/tools/accessible-color-palette-generator) by [Venngage](https://venngage.com/), an infographics creator:

![border](/pic/notes/Screenshot_2024-09-18_at_15.51.53.png)

Through a comment in a blog post, I also got pointed to [**Fette­Palette**](https://meodai.github.io/fettepalette/) by David Aerne. In German, that translates to “fat palette”), which is a cool implementation of an idea called “hue shifting”. David created a *lot* of small color applications, as you can see in his [portfolio](https://bento.me/meodai) – like [AutoAlbers](https://albers.elastiq.ch/), which you can see above. Very, very cool.
