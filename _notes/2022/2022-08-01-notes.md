August! Neat. Today I dealt with more hiring stuff – and then somebody made me aware that the R package [Farver](https://farver.data-imaginist.com/reference/convert_colour.html) converts colors from HEX to OkLCH the same as [oklch.evilmartians.io](https://oklch.evilmartians.io/#70,0.1,243,100), but differently than the official [OkLCH picker](https://bottosson.github.io/misc/colorpicker/#efb90b). 

A color like #efb90b has a lightness of 78 in the official picker, but 87 in the Evil Martians tool. The hue and chroma are also slightly different. 
 

![](/pic/notes/CleanShot_2022-08-01_at_18.25.392x.png)

![](/pic/notes/CleanShot_2022-08-01_at_18.26.182x.png)

Not sure what’s the reason for that. But definitely confusing.
