Hello world! While I wrote mostly into the void since mid-November, I now might (might!) have an audience. Maybe some of you are opening this page once and never again. Maybe some of you are checking in every few days or weeks. **Are dozens reading what I’m writing? Or zero? I don’t know**, because this is Notion, and no analytics are turned on for this page – on purpose.

Well, anyway – today was a day that felt great, thanks to you all. With the help of my coworkers Rose and Eddie I wrote and published the [**announcement of the book and these updates**](https://blog.datawrapper.de/book-about-colors-in-data-vis/) you’re reading right now. I also posted in on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/lisacmuth/status/1478784462397784069), and many of you were incredible nice and replied some nice words. Thank you! 

And I had a 30min call with another person who replied to the color survey and had a really nice chat with them about style guides and “running out of colors”. Like yesterday, **hearing thoughts from others on colors was very motivational** – but I got my third Covid vaccination at the other side of Berlin this afternoon, so instead of illustrating more color advice and doing more research, I sat in subways for a long time.
