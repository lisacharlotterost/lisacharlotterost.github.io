I didn’t get as much done as I wanted, but I did more research into backgrounds. And which kind of background is used by visualizations out there. And I stumbled upon a new tool while doing so: The **(OK)LCH Color Picker & Converter** by [Andrey Sitnik](https://sitnik.ru/)
and [Roman Shamin](https://twitter.com/romanshamin_en). It has really, really nice graphics showing 

- the highest possible **saturation** (chroma, top right) for each lightness and hue
- the possible **hues** for the selected saturation and lightness (bottom right)

It’s great to get an intuitive understanding for which hues can have which saturation at which lightness. Very, very neat.
