Today I helped two coworkers with their blog posts – they have nothing to do with color but I’m still very much looking forward to see them published. 

Besides that, I continued with my style guide blog post. And I looked at a looot of style guides. There’s [this list of style guides](https://policyviz.com/2016/11/30/style-guides/) that include data visualization guidelines that Jon Schwabish put together; and this [Google Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1F1gm5QLXh3USC8ZFx_M9TXYxmD-X5JLDD0oJATRTuIE/edit#gid=1679646668) that also collects a few of them...but I thought: There must be more. 

So I went on a quest to find them. And I failed. I looked at 80-page style guides with no mention of data visualizations. I looked at style guides from government organizations that create a a lot of charts with no mention of data visualizations. 

Oh well, I tried.
