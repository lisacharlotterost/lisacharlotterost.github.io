Such a good book. I wouldn't have read it without somebody on the internet (not sure who) recommending it, but I'm so glad I did. After two chapters, I really, really wanted to know how it continues. It's one of these books that made me miss it when I was not reading it.

It leaves a lot of open questions in the process: I often couldn't believe the book moved on without answering how Derek felt or thought about a certain interaction or situation. But it was a small annoyance compared with how incredible this story was. 

5/5.
