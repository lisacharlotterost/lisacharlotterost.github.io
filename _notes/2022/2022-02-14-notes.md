Today was one of these “outputting” days. I spent almost the full day focused on (almost!) finishing a blog post that I’ll publish this Wednesday – and it was great! I felt productive, and it was all very wonderful.

The main challenge today was figuring out the best way to phrase certain things. That’s always a challenge. I write a sentence, then delete parts of it and rewrite them, then start writing the whole sentence again differently in a new paragraph, then compare the two sentences and decide that the first one is better after all but that I need to bring the last part first. 

I’m no 100% happy with every phrasing yet...but I’ll need to gain some distance from it. I’ll look at it with fresh eyes again tomorrow or on Wednesday.
