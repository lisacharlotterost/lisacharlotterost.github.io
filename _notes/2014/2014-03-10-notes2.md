Printing Day! Did I ever mentioned that I don’t like printing? Things can go terribly wrong there - it’s just not as safe as the good old digital world. And it takes SO. MUCH. TIME. 

I brought the middle part to a printing shop today and let it print on gloss paper. It’s going to be ready to pick up tomorrow, and I’m really looking forward to see the end result. So far, so good.

The newspaper part is another story. The printing shop didn’t have newspaper paper, so I thought I’m going to print it myself. I bought nice paper (grey and 50g/qm), put it in our university printer and was prepared to be amazed. And I was - it looks really good - but it seems like the printer can’t swallow the paper after I already printed it on one side. I guess it’s to thin. Now I have ink all over my hands and face and clothes, because the paper always got stuck close to the ink cartridge. Weeeell.

I will start another trial tomorrow. It just HAS to be done. On the bright side, I really like the results from the failing printer.
