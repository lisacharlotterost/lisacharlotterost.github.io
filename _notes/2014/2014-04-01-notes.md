Today was a good day. It started with me finding excellent data about porn search terms on the [Data Insight blog of Pornhub](http://www.pornhub.com/insights/) (totally safe for work) and merging this data together so that I can start to create a graphic out of it. Than the “Projektbörse” followed: A room full (too full) of excited students listening to a few professors explaining their projects. I used the time mainly to thought of ideas for the realisation of the just mentioned porn graphic; and when the result will look as good as it does in my sketch book, than I’m happy. 

Then I got a good idea and put a border around the new-media part of my spreads in my new minimalistic magazine. I really like the 3D effect the double page had then. But my highlight today was a consultation with my supervisor M. - he said, the version I was working on in the last few days looks “like a science book”, a little bit dull and not minimalistic enough. And that I should look at swiss designers. And this feedback was PERFECT. It helped so much! I’m glad I went to him for a critique DURING my process, and now with the final magazine. He basically said: Change everything. And that’s exactly what I needed to hear. So my next magazine won’t look like the top image here, but like the bottom: One typeface on most pages (because that’s more minimalistic). More white space. Less colors.
