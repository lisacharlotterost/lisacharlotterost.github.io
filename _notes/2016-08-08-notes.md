Today I felt **GREAT**. It started yesterday night. I came home and thought: “Wow. The weather is as terrible as always. My activities today were nice but not mind-blowing. And still, I feel GREAT.”

And today it continued. I woke up at 8 and felt energized enough to do long-due stuff like cleaning up my apartment (ahem) and taking care of my **taxes**. I also had lots of fun watching **Olympics** (Gymnastics and Swimming) and was just amazed by the US Gymnastics team. They’re doing a great, great job. Wow. 

And I downloaded **Harry Potter and the Cursed Child** and started reading…and then I stopped. Well. I will continue, but I was a little bit disappointed that this book didn’t soak me into it and kept me fascinated for 5 hours straight. 

Aaaand in the evening I met **Jennings**, who’s in town again till Wednesday. We went to Tryst and worked, and that was great too. Biking through the DC night is actually really nice.

Yep. Good day.
