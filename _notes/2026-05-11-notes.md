I just discovered [Ferdio](https://ferdio.com/en/)'s ["Notebook on Infographics"](https://ferdio.com/en/insights/notebook-infographics/): Very useful, filterable lessons, and so (so!) beautifully animated.

And because you can also hire Ferdio for animation and presentations, they also offer
→ [The notebook on animation](https://ferdio.com/en/insights/notebook-animation/)
→ [The notebook on presentations](https://ferdio.com/en/insights/notebook-presentations/)

Until now, I only knew Ferdio because of their excellent [Data Viz Project](https://datavizproject.com/). It's great to see that they're investing so much time and love into projects that help the community.
