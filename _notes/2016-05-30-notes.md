Aaaand it’s Monday. I came home from my National Park trip yesterday evening, happy but a little bit exhausted: I’m so not used to hiking. Hiking for hours. Up and down. That was…something. My muscles are sore. Also, I’m not used to eating so much bread with almond butter and cookie butter as I did in the last three days. 

But it was super nice! We had extremely good weather and I saw some waterfalls and the other NPR people were super nice. 

Today I relaxed a lot. I went shopping, but besides that I was indulging in watching “Breaking Bad” and was drawing and was skyping with my mother and R and started the book “Quiet”. And I also worked for a few hours, because I left early on Friday. That went well! I finally got the data I wanted; I analyzed it the way I wanted to analyze it and then I put it in a R Markdown file as planned. Yeah.
