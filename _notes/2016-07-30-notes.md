**Portland, day 6,** and the 2nd and last day of the **SRCCON conference**. Great, great day. I got used to the noise level at the venue, and had far more conversations – although the sessions were not as exceptionally good as yesterday. Some highlights of the day: 

– I woke up very early and worked with my fellow fellow Dan from my favorite coffee shop in Portland. Great **scones** again. – I submitted both a **talk proposal f**or the Hack/Hackers Media Party in Buenos Aires and for the MozFest in London. – I **doodled** like crazy (see at the top) and had lots of fun doing so.– Several people came up to me and told me that the really liked my **blog** and Twitter account. And I met some people from Twitter which I haven’t met before.– We had a barbecue with the alumni fellows and we played **Werewolf** and I was the werewolf at both times.
