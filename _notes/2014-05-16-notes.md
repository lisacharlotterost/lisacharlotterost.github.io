It’s two days since my last blog post – and I have no excuse, actually. Well, I left the Macpool, my current home and paradise-like work place earlier in the night during the last days – because it’s really flowing these days. I feel like I have all current and future tasks under control, which is a nice feeling. 

On wednesday, we came together as the Arbeitsgruppe Abschlussklasse again (task force graduation class), and I prepared a presentation on the categorization between Information (=Ratio) and Communication (=Emotion) in relation with my old axis between minimalism vs. complexity. Really liked this idea. Man, I just like visual models so much. 

Also on wednesday, I got a job from Intern Magazine. Do you remember Intern Magazine? They featured me on their website a few months ago with reeeeeeally nice words. Now I’m doing a flowchart for the actual physical magazine: 

We’re going to be exploring internships through [#infographics](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23infographics&src=hash) in one feature of [#IssueTwo](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23IssueTwo&src=hash) with the dream team of [@MOSSLESS](https://twitter.com/MOSSLESS) & [@lisacrost](https://twitter.com/lisacrost)….

That’s cool.

And on the top you can see my progress. I designed a “facts sheet” for the porn story, lay out the EVE Online story and changed the porn actor story a little bit. I already finished 86 pages out of planned 120 pages, and I still have one week – so I feel like I’m doing well. But let’s see how I think in a few days! Because on the weekend, I won’t work. So I really only have 7 days left till my next and last deadline.
