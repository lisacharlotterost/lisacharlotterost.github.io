Good day today! Because I remembered a quote from a random book that goes “Spend one hour per day thinking about what you want to do at this day.” **Especially designers are threatened to do stuff without thinking.** Sometimes, thinking can help – and today was the perfect day to do so. 

I drew a big **page plan** with all the double pages I have and thought a lot about “diversity vs. sameness”, as explained yesterday. And then I decided that I want to bring some design-conceptual structure in the magazine. So now my mag functions like this: **Every column that’s close to the center of each page is old-media content** (because of the old type area, for example used by Tchichold) – **and everything that’s at the edge of the paper is new-media-online-community content.**

AND I won’t design the old-media content. I’d love to – I have some nice design ideas for them – but it would mean that the whole magazine would be super clustered. So I’m designing the old-media content in the same style as I designed the Bitcoins articles: I’ll google certain words from the articles and put appearing images from the search on the page. Meaning, **I “google-image” my article illustrations.** So what you can see above on the left side of the page isn’t finished yet; images are still missing. (On the right-hand side are two options for the opener of the The Sims Online story.)

Good day indeed. **More structure is always good.** I feel like I can estimate the amount of future work on the mag now – I couldn’t do this in the last days.
